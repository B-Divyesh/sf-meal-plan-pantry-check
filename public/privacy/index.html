How we handle your data

Effective August 28, 2026.

What stays on your device

Your recipes, ingredient lines, serving choices, pantry confirmations, and shopping checks are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. They are not sent to us. Exported backups and CSV files go only where you choose to save or share them.

License checks

If you buy or restore the Household Edition, the license token and a dated check result are stored in your browser. The token is sent to the Sociobot billing API only to check whether it is valid. Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record and processes purchase information under its own policies. We never receive card details.

What we do not use

We do not use advertising cookies, behavioral analytics, grocery affiliate tracking, remote recipe storage, URL scraping, or third-party fonts and scripts.

Your controls

Export your data from the planner. Remove this site’s data in your browser settings to erase saved recipes and the license from this device. You can raise questions through the public source repository.
