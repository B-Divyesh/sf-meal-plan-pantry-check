THE PANTRY LEDGER · LEGAL EDITION

Privacy, plain and local.

Effective August 28, 2026.

What stays on your device

Your recipes, ingredient lines, serving choices, pantry confirmations, and shopping checks are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. They are not sent to us. Exported backups and CSV files go only where you choose to save or share them.

License checks

If you buy or restore the Household Edition, the license token is stored in your browser and sent to the Sociobot billing API solely to check whether it is valid. Sociobot and Dodo are the merchant of record and process purchase information under their own policies. We never receive card details.

What we do not use

No advertising cookies, behavioral analytics, grocery affiliate tracking, remote recipe storage, URL scraping, or third-party fonts/scripts are used.

Your controls

Export your data from the recipe desk. Remove site data in your browser settings to erase the local ledger and license from this device. Questions can be raised through the project’s public source repository.
